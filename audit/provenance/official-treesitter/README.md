Snapshots of the official tree-sitter grammar highlight queries
(tree-sitter/tree-sitter-<lang>, fetched 2026-07-20). These serve as the
"fifth reference" in provenance review (see docs/PHILOSOPHY.md): they establish
structural distinctions recognized across modern grammars—addressing the
*existence* of syntax distinctions rather than their *color*—though they vary in
consistency across grammars (e.g., heuristics that misclassify SCREAMING_CASE
names as `@constructor`).
